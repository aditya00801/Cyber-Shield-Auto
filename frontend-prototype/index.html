Frequently asked questions

Does this UI currently protect my computer?

No. It is a frontend prototype and does not connect to collectors or execute response actions.

Are the dashboard numbers real?

No. Any visible incident counts and audit entries are illustrative sample data.

Can the login authenticate users?

Not yet. The login page only demonstrates form validation states and is not a security boundary.

What does CICIDS2017 support?

It supports network-flow experiments, not native endpoint process or authentication telemetry.

PROJECT OVERVIEW

Defensive security, with decisions you can explain.

CyberShield-Auto is an academic defensive security prototype designed to investigate suspicious activity, assess risk, and evaluate responses through explicit policy controls.

EARLY DEVELOPMENT

01 · Detect & explain

Validate events, surface suspicious patterns, and preserve evidence behind each finding.

02 · Assess risk

Keep severity, confidence, and context distinct for informed analyst review.

03 · Respond safely

Require policy approval, restricted actions, verification, and audit history.

Project status & scope
